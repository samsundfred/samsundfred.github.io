❤️ Relationship Explorer

Learn about different relationship structures, communication styles, and concepts related to consensual relationships. This website is educational and is designed to help people understand different relationship models. There is no single "correct" relationship style—healthy relationships depend on communication, respect, consent, and agreements that work for the people involved.
